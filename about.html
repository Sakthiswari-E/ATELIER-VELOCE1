About Atelier Véloce

Fourteen Years of
Architectural Serenity

Founded in 2012 by Julian Vance and Elena Rossi, Atelier Véloce emerged with a singular commitment: to rescue interior architecture from the churn of passing trends. We design private residences, penthouses, and bespoke modular kitchens where structural honesty and tactile luxury coexist seamlessly.

Our Architectural Ethos

Where Precision Meets Tactile Warmth

Every project begins with a 3D LiDAR laser scan of the site and a comprehensive lifestyle survey. We refuse standard catalogue fixtures, choosing instead to collaborate directly with stone quarries in Carrara and Tivoli, master joineries in Bavaria, and lighting artisans in Venice.

Full In-House Architectural, MEP & Joinery Documentation
Strict Single-Point Responsibility & Fixed Budget Contracts
10-Year Hardware Guarantee with Lifetime Blum Certification
