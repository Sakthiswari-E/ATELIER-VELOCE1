The 5-Stage Framework

Our Architectural Design Process

From initial discovery and millimeter 3D scans to white-glove champagne handover, our proven process eliminates construction uncertainty.

Stage 01

Discovery & Site Survey

Week 1 - 2

3D LiDAR laser scans, lifestyle questionnaire, structural feasibility and budget alignment.

Stage 02

Space Planning & Concepts

Week 3 - 5

Circulation optimization, tactile stone/wood moodboards, and 2D spatial distribution.

Stage 03

Photorealistic 3D BIM

Week 6 - 8

Daylight Kelvin studies, cabinetry detail drawings down to 0.5mm, and fixed Bill of Quantities.

Stage 04

Joinery & Execution

Week 9 - 18

Direct Italian stone quarrying, daily site engineer oversight, and 14 quality sign-off gates.

Stage 05

White-Glove Handover

Week 19 - 20

Environmental deep cleaning, art placement, lighting scene calibration, and 10-year warranty delivery.
