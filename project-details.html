Penthouse Architecture 2025 Handover

The Sovereign Penthouse

A light-flooded architectural sanctuary above the Tribeca skyline merging Japanese wabi-sabi calmness with Manhattan monumentalism.

Location
Tribeca, New York
Total Scale
5,400 sq.ft
Timeline
9 Months Turnkey
Lead Architect
Julian Vance, AIA
01 / The Architectural Challenge

Spatial Obstruction & Fragmented Light

The original floor plan consisted of awkward structural columns and fragmented drywall partitions that completely obstructed panoramic Hudson River vistas. Natural light failed to penetrate into the rear dining corridors.

02 / The Atelier Solution

Smoked Oak Colonnades & Open Vistas

We re-engineered structural load points into clad smoked-oak colonnades, opening a 60-foot contiguous living gallery flanked by an open cantilevered marble staircase, Lutron circadian lighting, and custom acoustic ceiling coffers.
